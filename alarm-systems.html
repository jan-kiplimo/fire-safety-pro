Fire Alarm Systems

Fire alarm systems detect signs of fire and warn occupants so they can evacuate promptly.

Main Components

  • Smoke detectors that detect smoke.
  • Heat detectors that respond to temperature changes.
  • Manual call points for raising an alarm.
  • Control panels that receive detector signals.
  • Sounders and visual alarms that alert occupants.

Testing and Maintenance

Arrange regular inspection and maintenance by qualified personnel. Keep detectors unobstructed and report system faults promptly.

What to Do When the Alarm Sounds

  1. Remain calm and follow the evacuation procedure.
  2. Use the nearest safe exit; avoid lifts during a fire.
  3. Go to the designated assembly point.
  4. Do not re-enter until authorized.

Never ignore a fire alarm. If a fire is suspected, evacuate and contact the appropriate emergency service.

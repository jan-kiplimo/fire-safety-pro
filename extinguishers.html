Fire Extinguishers

Fire extinguishers are essential safety devices used to control small fires during their early stages. Choosing the correct extinguisher and knowing how to use it can help protect lives and property.

Types of Fire Extinguishers

  • Water: Suitable for certain fires involving ordinary solid materials.
  • Foam: Suitable for certain solid-material and flammable-liquid fires.
  • CO₂: Commonly used for electrical equipment and some liquid fires.
  • Dry powder: Suitable for specific fire classes according to its label.
  • Wet chemical: Designed primarily for cooking-oil and fat fires.

How to Use an Extinguisher

  1. Raise the alarm and call emergency services.
  2. Only attempt to fight a small fire if you are trained and have a safe escape route.
  3. Use the extinguisher only if it is suitable for the type of fire.
  4. Follow the operating instructions on the extinguisher and keep your exit behind you.
  5. Leave immediately if the fire spreads or conditions become unsafe.

Inspection and Maintenance

Check extinguishers regularly for damage, accessibility, pressure indication where fitted, and inspection labels. Arrange servicing by qualified personnel according to applicable standards and manufacturer instructions.

Safety first: Never use water on energized electrical equipment or burning cooking oil. Evacuate and call the appropriate emergency service if there is any doubt.
